# MEDLINK Global - Healthcare Professional Migration Assessment Platform

**Transparent, Ethical Healthcare Workforce Migration Solutions**

MEDLINK Global connects healthcare professionals from the Indian subcontinent with international career opportunities through an AI-powered assessment and matching platform.

---

## 🎯 **Overview**

This interactive web application provides personalized migration roadmaps for healthcare professionals seeking international opportunities. Users complete a comprehensive 5-minute assessment and receive:

- **Personalized destination matching** across 9 countries
- **Detailed cost-benefit analysis** with realistic expense breakdowns
- **Timeline projections** from assessment to first paycheck
- **5-year financial forecasting** comparing international vs domestic earnings
- **Step-by-step roadmap** with licensing, credential, and visa requirements

---

## 👥 **Target Professions**

The platform serves three healthcare professional categories:

- **Registered Nurses** - GNM, BSc, Post-Basic BSc, MSc Nursing
- **Physicians** - MBBS, MD, MS, DNB, DM, MCh specialists
- **Allied Health** - Physiotherapists, Radiographers, Lab Technicians, Pharmacists, OT, Dietitians

## ✨ **Key Features**

### **Intelligent Matching Algorithm**
- Multi-factor scoring system (0-100 scale)
- Considers: English proficiency (CEFR A1-C2), clinical experience (Benner's expertise model), timeline goals, family situation
- Ranks destinations as Best/Strong/Good matches

### **Profession-Specific Pathways**
- Dynamic assessment questions tailored to each profession
- Nursing: Multi-select specializations (ICU, ER, OR, Pediatric, etc.) with expertise levels
- Physicians: Specialty tracking with residency/consultant experience tiers
- Allied Health: 10+ sub-disciplines with credential-specific requirements

### **Comprehensive Cost Analysis**
- Realistic expense breakdowns by destination
- Separate line items: Exams, Credentials, Language Training, Visa, Documentation
- Part-time work offset calculations (Germany, EU destinations)
- Range: ₹95K (UAE) to ₹520K (USA)

### **Visual Analytics**
- Timeline comparison charts (months to first paycheck)
- 5-year income projection graphs (destination vs India baseline)
- Interactive cost breakdowns with part-time work benefits

### **Language Proficiency Assessment**
- Full CEFR framework (A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2)
- IELTS/OET/PTE score tracking
- Multi-language capability tracking
- Part-time work eligibility alerts for language-learning destinations

## 📊 **Data Accuracy**

All cost estimates, salary projections, and licensing requirements are based on:
- Official regulatory body fee schedules (NMC, GMC, NMBI, ANMAC, etc.)
- Current visa application costs (2024-2025)
- Market salary data from recruitment agencies
- Language training costs from accredited providers

**Costs are conservative estimates** - actual expenses may vary by ±15% based on individual circumstances.
